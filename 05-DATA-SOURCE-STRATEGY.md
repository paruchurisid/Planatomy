# Data source strategy

## Source strategy

Start with a curated evidence sample before building broad collection. Obtain enough publishable material to connect a small set of aircraft occurrences to exact part identities and interpretable prices. Treat access, extraction, storage, public display, derived analytics, and export as separate permissions.

No source named here has been onboarded, licensed, authenticated, or approved for automated collection. Researching a public product page does not establish access to its underlying data. Source commercial terms and current automated-access policy must be checked during onboarding.

## What public technical material can establish

General dimensions and system descriptions can help orient educational geometry. They do not establish a complete interior component catalog or serial-specific installed configuration. An illustrated parts catalog can potentially support a part-to-assembly relationship, but only within its revision and applicability conditions and with suitable rights.

Supplier model tags are discovery hints. Listings may lack manufacturer scope, condition, quantity basis, or exact aircraft effectivity. Use them to find candidates, not to certify mappings. Search-engine snippets are pointers to sources, never sufficient pricing evidence on their own.

## Evidence tiers

| Tier | Evidence | Eligible use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Permissioned transaction record with identity and terms | Separate transaction analytics for the authorized audience |
| B | Supplier quote with recipient, validity, identity, and terms | Quoted-price view within its rights and validity |
| C | Explicit public or licensed asking price with interpretable terms | Asking-price observation and comparable asking summaries |
| D | Authoritative catalog identity or applicability without price | Part mapping or component metadata |
| E | General technical description or educational asset | Reviewed anatomy explanation or spatial reference |
| F | Unverified repost, snippet, ambiguous listing, or generated claim | Discovery queue only |

These tiers describe evidence roles, not a universal trust score. A transaction can be precise about price while providing poor applicability evidence. A technical catalog can be authoritative about identity while providing no commercial evidence.

## Initial feasibility sample

Build a manually reviewed candidate list of 30 component concepts across at least five systems. Prioritize discrete replaceable units and clearly scoped assemblies. For each, seek at least one identity reference and one legitimate path to numeric price evidence. Include hard cases such as exchange terms, multiple manufacturer namespaces, and quote-only offers.

Record acquisition time, review time, exact identity coverage, commercial-term completeness, source independence, update potential, and publication rights. The goal is to understand repeatability, not to collect the largest possible sample.

The gate passes when at least 20 exact identities can support publicly usable numeric observations, at least two independent supplier organizations contribute evidence, a licensed or commissionable visual approach exists, and sample mappings survive aviation review. This gate does not require a multi-seller estimate for every identity.

If it fails, choose among a narrower part set, another passenger-jet pilot, a data partnership, or an explicitly anatomy-only prototype while pricing remains in research. Record the reduced product claim rather than silently lowering evidence standards.

## Source onboarding record

Each source record includes owner organization, official domain, source type, business contact if needed, permitted access method, robots and terms review dates, authentication requirements, field availability, update frequency, retention limits, attribution text, audience restrictions, derived-data rights, export rights, contract expiry, and deletion obligations.

Record operational limits: request budget, concurrency, acceptable schedules, maximum response size, pagination method, retry policy, region constraints, and incident contact. Also record whether price is public, customer-specific, geographic, quantity-dependent, or accessible only through a quote workflow.

A source without a numeric price is still valuable for identity research. It should not be repeatedly crawled in hopes of obtaining an inaccessible field.

## Geometry acquisition

Rank acquisition paths as commissioned original geometry, explicitly licensed existing geometry with sufficient interior segmentation, permissioned engineering-derived material, and schematic shapes authored from legitimate references. Each needs a documented license and intended-publication check.

A model being downloadable or purchased for rendering does not establish permission to distribute its underlying mesh in a browser. Verify browser delivery and asset extraction implications. Do not assume flight-simulator assets, marketplace previews, OEM drawings, or fan models are reusable.

## Source independence

Track both website operator and underlying seller. One seller syndicating inventory through three websites is usually one supply-side evidence source, not three independent observations. Use verified ownership or syndication evidence where available and a conservative independence count when uncertain.

Do not use advertising rank, marketplace size claims, or search prominence as an evidence-quality weight. Treat source-reported verification status as a claim unless independently checked.

## Licensing and commercial negotiation questions

Before committing to a provider, request a permitted sample; clarify whether prices are asks, quotes, or transactions; determine whether historical changes are supplied; and ask whether public component-level statistics are allowed. Confirm whether source deletion must remove derived summaries, whether attribution is mandatory, and whether access continues for archived analyses after the agreement ends.

No outreach, account creation, purchase, or agreement execution is part of this documentation task.
